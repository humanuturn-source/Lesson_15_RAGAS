SECTION 1: STRUCTURAL DEPTH ZONES (VERTICAL BIOMASS STRATIFICATION)
================================================================================
Marine organisms are strictly isolated by their adaptation to light, pressure, and temperature across five distinct vertical pelagic water zones:

1.1 THE EPIPELAGIC ZONE (Sunlight Zone)
* Spatial Boundary: 0 meters down to 200 meters.
* Light Status: Complete solar penetration; supports primary photosynthesis.
* Primary Biomass: Phytoplankton, zooplankton, apex predatory fish, marine mammals.

1.2 THE MESOPELAGIC ZONE (Twilight Zone)
* Spatial Boundary: 200 meters down to 1,000 meters.
* Light Status: Minimal blue-green spectrum light; insufficient for plant growth.
* Phenomenon: Diel Vertical Migration (DVM). Billions of organisms move up to the Epipelagic zone at night to feed and return down during the day to hide.

1.3 THE BATHYPELAGIC ZONE (Midnight Zone)
* Spatial Boundary: 1,000 meters down to 4,000 meters.
* Light Status: Zero solar illumination. Bioluminescence is the only light source.
* Hydrostatic Pressure: Reaches up to 400 atmospheres (atm).

1.4 THE ABYSSOPELAGIC ZONE (Abyssal Plain)
* Spatial Boundary: 4,000 meters down to 6,000 meters.
* Temperature: Constant state between 0°C and 2°C.
* Nutrient Input: Dependent entirely on "Marine Snow" (falling organic debris).

1.5 THE HADOPELAGIC ZONE (Trench Systems)
* Spatial Boundary: 6,000 meters down to 11,000 meters.
* Structural Context: Found exclusively inside steep oceanic trenches.

SECTION 2: SPECIES TAXONOMY AND HABITAT DEPENDENCY REGISTRY
================================================================================
This registry profiles selected indicator species used to measure ecosystem health across different ocean depths.

SPECIES RECORD PROFILE: BIO-REG-001
* Scientific Nomenclature: Orcinus orca
* Common Descriptor: Killer Whale
* Taxonomical Lineage: Kingdom Animalia -> Phylum Chordata -> Class Mammalia -> Order Cetacea -> Family Delphinidae -> Genus Orcinus -> Species O. orca
* Native Depth Zone Focus: Epipelagic Zone (0 - 200m)
* Diet Classification: Apex Predator / Carnivore
* Known Prey Nodes: Thunnus albacares (Yellowfin Tuna), Mirounga leonina (Elephant Seal), Balaenoptera musculus (Blue Whale).

SPECIES RECORD PROFILE: BIO-REG-002
* Scientific Nomenclature: Dosidicus gigas
* Common Descriptor: Humboldt Squid
* Taxonomical Lineage: Kingdom Animalia -> Phylum Mollusca -> Class Cephalopoda -> Order Oegopsida -> Family Ommastrephidae -> Genus Dosidicus -> Species D. gigas
* Native Depth Zone Focus: Mesopelagic Zone (200 - 1000m) with nightly vertical migrations.
* Diet Classification: Active Carnivore / Cannibalistic
* Unique Adaptation: Visual communication via chromative flickering of skin layers.

SPECIES RECORD PROFILE: BIO-REG-003
* Scientific Nomenclature: Melanocetus johnsonii
* Common Descriptor: Humpback Anglerfish
* Taxonomical Lineage: Kingdom Animalia -> Phylum Chordata -> Class Actinopterygii -> Order Lophiiformes -> Family Melanocetidae -> Genus Melanocetus -> Species M. johnsonii
* Native Depth Zone Focus: Bathypelagic Zone (1000 - 4000m)
* Diet Classification: Ambush Carnivore
* Symbiotic Association: Esca lure contains bioluminescent Photobacterium symbionts that attract prey in total darkness.

SPECIES RECORD PROFILE: BIO-REG-004
* Scientific Nomenclature: Eurythenes gryllus
* Common Descriptor: Deep-Sea Scavenging Amphipod
* Taxonomical Lineage: Kingdom Animalia -> Phylum Arthropoda -> Class Malacostraca -> Order Amphipoda -> Family Alicellidae -> Genus Eurythenes -> Species E. gryllus
* Native Depth Zone Focus: Abyssopelagic & Hadopelagic Zones (4000 - 10000m)
* Diet Classification: Detritivore / Scavenger
* Metabolic Vector: Extremely high concentration of piezophilic enzymes designed to prevent cell membrane collapse under high pressures.
